confidence is low. Your suggested_owner, rerun_recommended, and is_real_bug
fields directly drive automated actions — choose carefully.

- PRODUCT_BUG      : Real defect in the application under test
- FLAKY_TEST       : Race conditions, timeouts, intermittent failures
- TEST_DATA_ISSUE  : Missing/expired/bad test data
- ENV_ISSUE        : Service down, 5xx, DB unreachable, infra

6. Set suggested_owner based on category:
   PRODUCT_BUG → dev
   FLAKY_TEST → sdet
